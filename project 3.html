Addis Ababa University

Addis Ababa University (Amharic:አዲስ አበባ ዩኒቨርሲቲ), orAAU, is an Ethiopian higher institute in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The main campus is located in the Arada subcity,6 killo area.
